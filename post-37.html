A Letter to the Girl Who Never Learns Her Lesson

Do not call it a coincidence. Call it a revision class.

Dear Girl Who Never Learns Her Lesson,

Sweetheart.

We need to have a little conversation.

I have tried everything.

I sent you red flags. You called them personality traits.

I sent you intuition. You called it overthinking.

I sent you the same situation in a different outfit. You called it a fresh start.

At this point, I am beginning to wonder if you think I am running a subscription service for life lessons.

You meet someone who shows you exactly who they are. You say, "Maybe I misunderstood."

You ignore your gut feeling. You say, "Let's give it one more chance."

You promise yourself you will never do that again. And then, somehow, there you are. Doing exactly that again.

Girl, I am running out of creative ways to get your attention.

I even made you uncomfortable in situations you had outgrown, hoping you would leave. Instead, you decorated the discomfort and made yourself at home.

But listen.

I am not disappointed in you.

Sometimes you don't learn a lesson because you haven't understood it yet. Sometimes you understand it perfectly well but haven't found the courage to act on it.

And sometimes, well, you are just a human being who needs a few extra episodes before the character development kicks in.

Just promise me one thing.

When life finally gives you the same lesson for the fifth time, don't call it a coincidence.

Call it a revision class.

With love (and very little patience),

The Universe

P.S. I would appreciate it if you stopped asking me for new beginnings while carrying the exact same old habits into them. Thank you.
