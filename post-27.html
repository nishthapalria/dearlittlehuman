A Letter From Your Future Self

Monday has heard this speech 46 times. Stop saving your life for a better version of yourself.

Dear You,

Love, Your Future Self.

Girl. We need to talk.

And before you say "Monday se pakka," I would like to remind you that Monday has heard this speech 46 times.

I have been waiting for you.

Remember that day you decided you were going to become THAT girl? The one who wakes up early, reads books, drinks her water, has a beautiful wardrobe, speaks intelligently, works out, has a successful career and somehow never has a single unfinished task?

Cute presentation. Very convincing.

What happened afterwards?

You watched 37 glow-up videos, saved 18 Pinterest boards, downloaded two habit-tracking apps and spent three hours choosing the perfect journal.

And then you got tired.

FROM PLANNING TO BECOME A PERSON WHO DOES THINGS.

Honestly, the irony is doing overtime.

You keep saying you want to be more confident, more articulate, more disciplined, more adventurous, more knowledgeable.

Darling, at this point, even your vision board is wondering when the actual construction will begin.

And this obsession with waiting for the perfect moment?

The perfect Monday. The perfect mood. The perfect outfit. The perfect version of yourself who will magically wake up one morning with excellent habits and a five-year plan.

I hate to break it to you, but she is not coming to rescue you.

You have to become her.

And no, you don't need another self-improvement video explaining the importance of consistency. You have consumed enough information to start a small university.

You need to actually do something.

Read three pages. Go for that walk. Apply for that job. Say what you mean. Wear the outfit you've been saving for a special occasion.

PLEASE STOP SAVING YOUR LIFE FOR A BETTER VERSION OF YOURSELF.

You know what I find particularly funny?

You keep imagining how wonderful your life will look once you have transformed yourself.

But you are treating the person who has to build that life like an unpaid intern.

She is you, by the way.

The current, slightly confused, occasionally lazy, frequently distracted you.

She deserves a chance too.

So here is my proposal.

Let's stop announcing our new personalities.

Let's quietly do one thing today.

No dramatic declarations. No 5 AM club. No purchasing stationery in the name of personal growth.

Just one small promise. Kept.

And another tomorrow.

I'll be here when you arrive.

Hopefully not after another 47 Pinterest boards.

With love, mild disappointment and an unreasonable amount of faith in you,

Your Future Self.

P.S. You don't need to become a completely different woman. I quite like the original. She just needs to get off the couch sometimes.
