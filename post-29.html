A Letter From Your Body

We have been meaning to thank you. Yes, all of us.

Dear Girl,

Love, Your Body.

We have been meaning to thank you.

Yes, ALL of us.

Your legs, for instance, would like you to know that they thoroughly enjoy your random dancing sessions in the kitchen.

Although they would appreciate a little warning before you suddenly decide you are in a music video.

Your cheeks are also very grateful.

Apparently, you still blush when someone compliments you, giggle at silly jokes and smile at dogs like you have just met a celebrity.

Your eyes? Oh, they are obsessed with you.

They love how you still stop to look at pretty sunsets, get excited about fairy lights and stare at Christmas decorations even when it's February.

Your hands would like to thank you for all the unnecessary little purchases you make because something is "just too cute."

We have approximately 14 decorative things that serve absolutely no purpose, but apparently they bring you joy.

And your heart?

Well, your heart is particularly fond of the fact that you still get excited about things.

A birthday cake. A surprise parcel. A new café. A silly little plan.

You haven't completely surrendered to the idea that being an adult means being permanently serious, permanently busy and permanently concerned about electricity bills.

Although, between us, you should probably start checking those bills.

But here is what we really wanted to say.

Thank you for not making us grow up faster than necessary.

Thank you for still laughing loudly, getting excited over little things, making silly faces, dancing badly and finding reasons to celebrate an ordinary Tuesday.

The world will occasionally ask you to be more serious, more practical, more sensible and more grown-up.

Please do that when necessary.

But don't confuse growing up with becoming boring.

We have absolutely no interest in becoming a collection of tired body parts attached to someone who only gets excited about tax refunds.

Keep your wonder. Keep your ridiculous enthusiasm. Keep finding magic in the smallest things.

We quite like living this life with you.

With love, giggles and the occasional backache,

Your Body.

P.S. Your knees would like to remind you that being childlike does not mean you can jump off the sofa like you are seven. We are 30. Please behave.
