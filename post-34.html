A Letter to My Dear Sensitive Girl

From your heart, which was not hired to host a 24-hour emotional investigation channel.

Dear Girl,

Love, Your Heart.

Can we have a little chat?

First of all, I love you. Very much.

But I would also like to know why we are treating every minor inconvenience like the season finale of a television drama.

Someone spoke slightly differently to you?

We are hurt.

Someone forgot to ask how your day was?

We are hurt.

Someone cancelled plans?

We are hurt, but now we're also pretending we didn't want to go anyway.

Someone said something five years ago?

Excellent. Let's bring that up at 11:46 PM. I was getting ready to sleep, but apparently we are having a retrospective.

Darling, I am a heart. I pump blood. I was not hired to host a 24-hour emotional investigation channel.

And you know what else?

You feel EVERYTHING.

A kind stranger remembers your coffee order and suddenly you want to write them a thank-you letter.

A friend sends you a thoughtful message and you save it like it's a historical document.

You hear a sad song and suddenly you are looking out of the car window like you have just lost the love of your life.

I mean, the emotional range in this household is impressive.

But here is where I need you to listen.

Just because you feel something deeply doesn't mean you have to do something about it immediately.

You can be hurt without sending a 14-paragraph explanation.

You can be disappointed without deciding someone doesn't care about you.

You can love someone without making their happiness your full-time responsibility.

And you can forgive someone without giving them another opportunity to ruin your Tuesday.

Also, please stop apologising for having feelings.

You are not a customer complaint that needs to be resolved.

I actually quite like that you notice little things. That you remember details. That you get excited over thoughtful gestures. That you care when someone is having a difficult day.

Please don't lose that.

Just perhaps stop handing everybody the remote control to your emotional television.

Not everyone deserves front-row seats to every feeling you experience.

So here is my little request.

Keep feeling. Keep caring. Keep getting ridiculously excited about small things.

But maybe, just maybe, let some things pass without inviting them to live rent-free in your chest for the next six business months.

I have other responsibilities, you know.

With love, a little exhaustion and absolutely no plans to become emotionally unavailable,

Your Heart.

P.S. That person who hurt your feelings yesterday? I would like to remind you that we still have a perfectly good weekend ahead. Please don't cancel it on my behalf.
