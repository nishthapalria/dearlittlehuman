A Letter to the Hopeless Romantic

Keep your soft heart. Just give it a security system.

Dear Hopeless Romantic,

Love, sweetheart.

Can we talk?

I have watched you turn a two-minute conversation into a 12-episode romantic series. Someone remembers your coffee order and suddenly you are wondering what your wedding playlist would sound like.

You see couples holding hands and smile. You hear an old Bollywood song and stare out of the window like someone has just left you at a railway station.

You have a whole imaginary relationship with a person who hasn't even learned your last name.

And honestly? I find you adorable.

I made you with a heart that notices little things. The way someone's eyes light up when they talk about something they love. The extra five minutes of a conversation. The handwritten notes. The unexpected flowers. The kind of affection that doesn't need an announcement.

Please don't lose that.

But darling, a small request from the management upstairs:

Stop turning every person who shows you basic kindness into a potential love story.

Not everyone who smiles at you is your plot twist.

And please, stop thinking that because you haven't experienced the kind of love you dream about, something is missing from your life.

I have not forgotten about you.

But while you're waiting for someone to bring you flowers, buy yourself some. While you are waiting for someone to take you on a lovely date, go somewhere beautiful. Wear that dress. Order the dessert. Play the ridiculously romantic song.

Your life is not a waiting room for romance.

And one more thing: when love does arrive, I hope it feels less like solving a mystery and more like coming home.

Until then, keep your soft heart. Just give it a security system.

With love, The Universe

P.S. I created billions of people. Please stop deciding someone is your soulmate because they have nice hands. We need to be a little more thorough.
