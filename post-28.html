A Letter From Your Mirror

I am a mirror, not a comparison calculator. Please let the woman drink her coffee.

Dear Girl,

Love, Your Mirror.

Can we PLEASE have a conversation?

Because I am getting tired of this little morning ritual.

You wake up, come stand in front of me, look at yourself and say, "Hmm. Not bad."

THREE SECONDS LATER.

You see a girl on Instagram.

And suddenly I am being questioned like I have committed a crime.

Why is her skin so clear? Why does her hair look like that? Why does she have such a nice wardrobe? Why does she look so effortlessly beautiful?

HELLO? I WAS JUST HERE.

And what exactly am I supposed to do when you start zooming into somebody else's photograph and comparing their entire existence to your face at 8:30 in the morning?

I am a mirror, not a comparison calculator.

You do realise you have a very strange habit, right?

You can receive ten compliments and spend the entire evening thinking about one person's slightly unusual expression.

Someone says you look lovely. You smile.

Someone doesn't notice your new haircut. Suddenly, we're reconsidering our entire appearance.

A girl walks into a room looking confident, and instead of thinking, "She looks lovely," you start wondering what is wrong with YOU.

Darling, must every beautiful woman become a personal performance review?

And don't even get me started on the imaginary competition.

You have been competing with women who don't know you exist, women you have never met and women whose lives you have constructed entirely from 12 Instagram pictures.

Somewhere, a stranger is simply enjoying her coffee, completely unaware that she has been appointed your unofficial life rival.

PLEASE LET THE WOMAN DRINK HER COFFEE.

Here is something I wish you understood.

You don't have to be the prettiest person in the room to enjoy being in it.

You don't have to be the most interesting, the most successful, the most fashionable or the most effortlessly confident.

And you certainly don't have to find something wrong with yourself every time you notice something lovely about someone else.

There is enough room for everybody to be beautiful, darling.

Even you. Especially you.

So tomorrow, when you come stand in front of me, can we try something different?

Look at yourself without conducting an inspection.

Wear the lipstick. Take the picture. Go out. Stop waiting until you look like somebody else's idea of perfect.

And occasionally, when you see another beautiful woman, just let her be beautiful.

You don't have to become her.

I have been looking at you for years. Trust me, there's a rather lovely person standing here already.

With love and absolutely no intention of entertaining another comparison session,

Your Mirror.

P.S. I am reflective, not responsible for your self-esteem. Please stop shouting at me.
WhatsApp
